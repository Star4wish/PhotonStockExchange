# Changelog / 更新日志

## 0.1.1

中文：
- MOD 改名为 **光子证券交易所**，游戏内建筑名称同步更新。
- 界面支持中英双语：游戏语言为中文时显示中文，其它语言自动显示英文。
- 行情面板点击星系名称，可在星图上添加 / 取消该星系的导航线（同一目标再点一次取消）。
- 使用新的 MOD 图标。
- 新增物品图标：能量币为蓝边白色塑料币面+黄色闪电；交易所为金色分馏塔图标。

English:
- Renamed to **Photon Stock Exchange**; the in-game building name is updated too.
- UI is now bilingual: Chinese when the game language is Chinese, English otherwise.
- Clicking a system name in the market panel toggles its navigation marker on the starmap (click again to clear).
- New mod icon.
- New item icons: energy coin (blue-rimmed white coin with a yellow lightning bolt) and exchange (gold-tinted fractionator icon).

## 0.1.0

中文：
- 首个正式版本：太阳帆 ↔ 能量币按各星系汇率即时兑换，可切换「卖出币 / 买入币 / 兑换光子」三种模式。
- 分馏塔外观建筑，两侧传送带进货、中间传送带出货，无延迟。
- 星系间价格不同，价格每 10 分钟刷新，买卖各收 2% 印花税。
- `Alt+3` 行情面板（全星系最便宜/最贵排行）、`Alt+4` 当前星系 K 线，均可在按键设置中改绑。
- 交易所需要通电；切换模式与拆除建筑都会退还仓内物品；仓内物品随存档保存。

English:
- First release: instant solar sail ↔ energy coin exchange at each system's own rate, with three modes (sell coins / buy coins / buy photons).
- Fractionator-style building: side belts feed in, middle belt outputs, no delay.
- Per-system prices, refreshed every 10 minutes, 2% stamp duty on both sides.
- `Alt+3` market panel (cheapest/dearest systems), `Alt+4` candle chart of the current system; both rebindable.
- The exchange needs power; switching modes and demolishing refund buffered items; the buffer is saved with the game.
